# Made in Null

### Experimental products, built anywhere.

We are **MIN** — an independent team of engineers and product makers.

We explore uncertain ideas, turn the promising ones into working systems, and take them all the way to real users. We are not tied to one industry, one platform, or one place. A product can begin anywhere. It only has to solve something real, be technically honest, and earn the right to exist.

## What we do

- Explore overlooked problems and unconventional product ideas.
- Build end-to-end experiments instead of presentation-only concepts.
- Combine product discovery, design, and engineering in one process.
- Turn successful experiments into reliable, maintainable products.
- Extract reusable tools and foundations from what we learn.

## How we work

- **Product and engineering belong together.** Feasibility shapes the idea; user value shapes the system.
- **Reality is the test.** We prefer a working prototype in someone's hands to certainty on a whiteboard.
- **Small teams should have a large surface area.** We automate the routine and keep humans focused on judgment.
- **“Works normally” is the baseline.** The product still needs a reason to be remembered.
- **Experiments are allowed to fail.** Their job is to leave us knowing more than we did before.

## Why “Made in Null”?

Every product starts in the same state: it does not exist yet.

`MIN` means **Made in Null** — built from an empty state, without inherited answers or artificial boundaries. It can also mean **Maybe It's Nothing**. Until an experiment meets reality, intellectual honesty requires both possibilities.

```text
null -> question -> experiment -> product
```

Some of our work is still behind closed doors. Public repositories will appear when they are ready to be useful beyond our own team.
